# Footage and sharing

The original choreography, frame images and labels are supplied for coursework on the team's project. The three additional videos are local copies used for visual testing. The project does not establish redistribution permission for their video or music.

- [Wicked Games](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CbpSMoPItu0)
- [onare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YoF0XeYhPL0)
- [Dernek](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BorT97NOmQ)

Before copying footage or frames to a repository shared outside the team or course, verify the permissions with the footage owner and course requirements. Track IDs are session-local association numbers. The tool supports review of stage positions and does not identify people by name.
