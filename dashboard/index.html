How alignment is scored

Each line is fitted to reported contacts. Its score starts at 100% and drops as the average perpendicular distance increases, reaching 0% at 6% of the stage width. Overall alignment averages the detected line scores. Hollow points are tracked estimates and do not contribute to a line. Two dots can define a perfect line, so use a higher minimum when judging group alignment.
